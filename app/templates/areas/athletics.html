From EADA, the Equity in Athletics Disclosure Act collection — a different federal survey from the IPEDS data behind the other areas. IPEDS records only whether a school belongs to the NCAA, not who plays or what it costs. Athletes are counted once each, so someone running cross country and track is one person here. Undergraduate enrolment is EADA's own count, used so the share is internally consistent rather than mixing two surveys. Revenues and expenses are omitted deliberately: every institution reports them as equal, because institutional support is booked as revenue, so the survey cannot show whether a programme runs at a loss.
