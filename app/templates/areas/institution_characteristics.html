A calendar marked (IPEDS YYYY) is that school's most recent report, not this year's — IPEDS has not finished publishing everyone's newest year yet. Location, control and size backfill the same way without a mark: they don't meaningfully change year to year for a school that already exists. Setting is NCES's locale for the school's own address, not its metro area — Cambridge, MA reads as a midsize city even though it sits inside greater Boston. Student:faculty ratio counts every instructional faculty member against every student, full- and part-time alike — it is a rough proxy for how much personal attention is realistic, not a class-size guarantee. Religious affiliation of “None” means IPEDS recorded the school as having no affiliation — a real answer, not a missing one. Top fields of study are each field's share of that school's own completions across all award levels (by primary major, all races and sexes combined) in the year shown next to the row — on its own window, not the year the rest of this table is showing; the total a share is taken against is IPEDS's own reported total, not our sum of the rows shown. Founded and Motto are the only two fields on this page that are not IPEDS — both come from Wikidata, joined on the same IPEDS ID Wikidata records for each school, and carry no publication year of their own. A missing motto means Wikidata has none on file, not that the school lacks one.
