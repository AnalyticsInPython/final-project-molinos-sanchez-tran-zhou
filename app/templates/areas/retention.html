Pell gap is completion for students on no federal aid minus completion for students on a Pell grant — a proxy for how a school does by students who arrived without money. Spread by race is the distance between the best and worst reported group. We compute both; IPEDS publishes neither, and the headline rate it does publish conceals both. Completion is measured at 150% of normal time, so six years for a four-year degree. Groups with fewer than {{ 30 }} students in the cohort are left out, and two categories are excluded from the race spread entirely — international students and students whose ethnicity went unrecorded describe a registrar's filing rather than a background a student would claim.
