Who you are

A username is all we ask for. There is no password — anyone who knows your username can open this profile, so keep it to things you would not mind a classmate seeing.

Where you live

This is the answer that moves the numbers most. A public university charges in-state and out-of-state students very differently — Michigan is $17,193 against $55,097 for the same year — so without your state we cannot tell you which price is yours.

What a year has to cost

Net price is published by family income band. Pick yours and the comparison shows what families like yours actually paid, instead of a sticker price almost nobody pays.

Your scores

A test score can be set against each school's published 25th–75th percentile range, so you can see where you sit. Bear in mind many schools went test-optional — at some, a quarter of admitted students submitted a score at all.

GPA is saved for your own reference only. IPEDS publishes no admitted GPA for any institution, so there is nothing honest to compare it against.
